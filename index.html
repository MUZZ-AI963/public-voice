Your right to sound it out — anonymously. When a government or agency publication does not stack with past public information, you authenticate it here and put an explain-ask on their desk. Primary sources only. No private home or phone. No classified material. Not legal advice.

How and why

Why: People are entitled to be lawfully informed from public records. Institutions hold the microphone; people hold the loss and the silence. This free desk turns a public claim into a checkable pack and puts it on the subject’s desk to explain — you can stay Anonymous.

How:

  1. Name the concern and the public door.
  2. Paste the claim with its primary-source URL.
  3. Set the silence clock or the counter-source.
  4. Write the people’s cost. Authenticate against the sources (AI assist later; sources still required).
  5. Copy the Send pack (Draft · not lodged). Seal as Anonymous if you want. Export when the checklist passes.
