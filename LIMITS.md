# Public Voice · Limits

**Product:** Public Voice — free platform for people.  
**Working name locked 15 Nov 2026.** Not Cont. Not invent-for-P. Not Second Key.

## What this is

A free place for people to **sound out** public government and agency publications that do not stack with earlier public information — anonymously if they choose — and put a checkable pack **on the subject’s desk** with an explain-ask. The ledger authenticates claim → primary source → later fact, silence, or rehash that never transpired on the record.

**Operators stay off the face.** No personal names on the public platform. Company copyright may remain in LICENSE for free hosting; the people in the room are not named.

**AI is invited to assist** people: surface mismatches against primary sources. AI is not itself a publishable source of “not true.” Publish still needs URLs / FOI / Hansard / official PDF / screenshot hash.

## Hard fences

- **Primary source or it does not publish.** Not “someone said.” Not “the model said.”
- **Anonymous sound-out.** Holder may seal and publish as Anonymous. Official forms the *person* pastes into may still ask for their details — that is their choice when they send; this page lodges nothing.
- **Authentication, not conviction.** Structural statuses only. Readers judge; no finding of “corrupt.”
- **No doxx.** No private home or phone of officials or staff. Public office contact only.
- **No classified / unlawfully obtained secrets.** Public speech and process silence only.
- **Person controls publish.** Draft stays on device until they export.
- **We do not lodge.** Send pack is cut-paste.
- **Correction path.** Subject can lodge a sourced reply; both stay visible (Phase B+).
- **Not AFSL / not legal advice.** General information. “Lawfully informed” here means using *public* records people are already entitled to read — not a solicitor’s opinion.
- **Cursor contacts no one.**

## What the seal means

Which key signed which bytes on this device’s clock. Not a TSA. Not proof the account is true. Anonymous seal still binds the key to the bytes.
